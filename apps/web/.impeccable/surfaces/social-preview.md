# Orbe social sharing preview

Mode: Persuade. A person sees a shared Orbe URL in Discord or another social service and should immediately recognize the product and its purpose. The existing graphite/coral identity and Plus Jakarta Sans are pinned by the current landing page. This is a narrow brand asset and metadata extension, not a new visual world.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make a shared link carry the same recognizable collection-first identity as the site, with a readable image at small embed sizes.

OWN-WORLD: Reuse the orbital SVG, graphite ground, white type, coral emphasis and self-hosted Plus Jakarta Sans. No generated illustration, invented product UI or new palette.

STORY: Recognize Orbe, read “Sua coleção. Na sua órbita.”, understand music/video/playlists, then follow the shared link.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 1200×630 static image: small brand lockup at the upper left; a large two-line headline in the left two-thirds; one large, intact orbital mark on the right; a quiet baseline with product purpose and the public domain. All text and logo remain inside generous safe margins. Inspect at 1200px and at a 400px embed width.

FORM: Code-led, precisely scoped extension of existing brand assets, matching the user's large-image React link-preview reference. No seed or comp tournament is required for this narrow existing-identity artifact. Render a reproducible HTML composition to PNG; social metadata lives in the initial HTML, accessible without JavaScript.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
